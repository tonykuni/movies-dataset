# Investment workspace visual UAT

## Central desktop 1440px

The central UI renders as a compact warm-light investment console. The watchlist, relative-performance chart, and risk lens appear in one horizontal analysis band below the existing KPI row. The sidebar, sticky topbar, and sticky footer remain aligned with the existing UCC treatment. The Seaborn-inspired blue/teal/amber/coral semantic accents are visually distinct without depending on red/green alone.

## Central mobile 390px

The layout collapses to a two-column KPI grid and a single-column investment workspace. The market controls wrap cleanly, and the watchlist stays readable without horizontal page overflow. The chart and risk lens continue below the fold as expected; the fixed footer remains visible. No visible clipping or broken typography was observed in the first viewport.

## SYNCHRONIZER desktop 1440px

The local-only synchronizer keeps a compact, dense light layout with two equal top panels, wide template and analytics panels, and the fixed footer. The new investment template is available in the industry dropdown; its visual panel remains consistent with the UCC treatment.

## SYNCHRONIZER mobile 390px

The hero actions wrap into a readable two-column grid, status cards stack into a single column, and the fixed footer remains visible. The layout remains serverless and does not introduce horizontal page overflow.
