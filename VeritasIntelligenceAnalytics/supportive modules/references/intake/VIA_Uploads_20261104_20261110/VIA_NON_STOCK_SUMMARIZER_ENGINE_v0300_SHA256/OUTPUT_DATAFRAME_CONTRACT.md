# NON-STOCK SUMMARIZER v0300 — Output DataFrame Contract

## 1. REPORT_SUMMARY — One Report = One Row

`REPORT_ID | REPORT_DATE | FILE_NAME | REPORT_TYPE | SOURCE | ANALYST_AUTHOR | MAIN_STORY | MAIN_INDUSTRY | TITLE | POINT_1 | POINT_2 | POINT_3 | POINT_4 | DIRECTION | HORIZON | COMPANY_COUNT | TOPIC_COUNT | EVIDENCE_COUNT | LAYOUT_ENGINE | TEXT_REPAIR_ENGINE | NLP_ENGINE | STATUS | DETAIL_REF`

## 2. TOPIC_DETAIL — One Topic / Physical Message = One Row

`REPORT_ID | TOPIC_ID | SOURCE_UNIT | TOPIC_TITLE | STORY_L1 | INDUSTRY | TITLE | POINT_1 | POINT_2 | POINT_3 | POINT_4 | DIRECTION | HORIZON | TICKERS | GLOBAL_ANCHORS | EVIDENCE_SIDS | NLP_HINT | STATUS`

For Messenger/LINE, a physical message is deliberately kept as one row; a long Call Memo is summarized into the four fixed points rather than exploding into dozens of rows.

## 3. COMPANY_DETAIL — One Company × Report = One Row

`REPORT_ID | TICKER | COMPANY_NAME | STORIES | INDUSTRY | RATING | TARGET_PRICE | TITLE | POINT_1 | POINT_2 | POINT_3 | POINT_4 | EVIDENCE_SIDS | STATUS`

## 4. EVIDENCE_DETAIL — Full drill-down

`REPORT_ID | CLAIM_ID | EVIDENCE_SID | PAGE | ATTRIBUTION | IMPORTANCE | DIRECTION | STORIES | TICKERS | DRIVERS | RELATIONS | PERIODS | TEXT`
