M December 2, 2025 04:40 PM GMT Bizlink | Asia Pacific Read-across from Credo's FY2Q26 Results Positive implications for Bizlink from Credo's strong FY2Q26 results, upbeat FY3Q26/FY26 guidance, and comments on its interconnect solutions FY2Q26 (ended October 2025) results:
• Credo (CRDO.US, not covered) reported revenue of US$268mn (+20% QoQ/ +272% YoY), exceeding the high-end of its guidance at US$230-240mn.
• Non-GAAP GM was 67.7% (+0.1ppt QoQ; guidance 64-66%) and net profit was US$128mn (+30% QoQ/+943% YoY).
• Four customers each contributed more than 10% of its revenue in FY2Q26, i.e., 42%, 24%, 16%, and 11%, respectively.
FY3Q26 and FY26 outlook:
• Credo guided FY3Q26 revenue to be US$335-345mn (+27% QoQ/+152% YoY at the mid-point, above consensus at US$248mn), non-GAAP GM at 64-66%
(vs. consensus at 64.9%) and opex at US$68-72mn, leading to revenue growth of more than 170%, non-GAAP opex growth of more than 50%, and non-GAAP NPM at ~45% for FY26.
• Management expects to see four hyperscaler customers above the 10%
revenue contribution threshold in FY26, with a fifth starting to contribute initial revenues in FY2Q26 and a skew toward FY2H26.
• It said it is on track for PCIE AEC design wins in FY26 and meaningful production revenue in FY27.
• On top of strong growth from AECs (Active Electrical Cables), Credo sees ALCs (Active LED Cables) being one of the next growth drivers in the coming years. It targets first product sampling in FY27 and initial revenue ramp in FY28. It expects TAM for ALC to be at least twice as large as that for AEC, as the former can deliver the same reliability and power profile up to 30 meters of transmission distance.

• We see positive implications for Bizlink from Credo's continuous ramp of AEC in scale-out networks now, and scale-up networks in the near future, as well as ALCs in the longer term,
