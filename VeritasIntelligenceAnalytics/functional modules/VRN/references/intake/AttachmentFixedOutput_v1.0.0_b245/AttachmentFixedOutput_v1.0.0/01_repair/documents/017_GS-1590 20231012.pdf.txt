Airtac International Group (1590.TW): Pneumatic demand to remain solid despite low season; Buy
Goldman囤ity Sachs I Research AirTAC reported September revenue of RMB591mn (+1% MoM, +24% YoY) / NT$2,592mn (+1% MoM, +22% YoY), leading 3Q23 revenue at NT$7,564mn (-5%
QoQ, +24% YoY), which was roughly in-line with GSe/BBG consensus of NT$7,495mn/NT$7,538mn. We saw linear guide revenue continue to grow MoM from July to September and make the overall linear guide revenue to reach ~RMB130mn, which is much higher than the 2Q linear guide revenue of ~RMB90mn. Moreover, pneumatic market demand performance was also solid while we started to see better industrial customers’ confidence on bottoming macro economics, leading to better investment demand in pneumatics. In September, energy lighting continued to be the strongest segment in terms of YoY growth (+100% YoY), followed by packaging / general machinery/ electronics (+25% YoY), and machine tool (+15% YoY) while textile revenue remains flat YoY in September.
However, both battery (-5% YoY) and automotive (-8% YoY) revenue declined YoY in September.

However, we expect both pneumatic and linear guide’s daily revenue will grow MoM. For pneumatics, which the company will not get constrained by inventories issues, will continue to recover on its daily revenue due to the bottoming demand environment in both infrastructure market (see here)
and property market (here) supported by government’s stimulus/loosening policy. For linear guide, customer expansion and ramp-up of production should help the company to grow, despite the ongoing difficult demand condition in machine tools customers.
