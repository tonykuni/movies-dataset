Mitac (3706.TW): AI liquid cooled rack showcased; from networking to AI servers; Buy
We reiterate our positive view on Mitac, given it is riding on the diversifying chipset platform trend in AI servers, which provides customers higher customization, flexibility, and reducing reliance on single chipset supplier. The company’s 10M25 revenues was strong at 94% YoY, driven by networking racks for AI servers, while in coming years, we expect its AI servers revenues contribution to reach up to 16% / 28% / 35% in 2026-28E, riding on the diversifying AI servers trend, and the company’s capacity expansion across US, Taiwan, and Vietnam. The company’s capacity expansion in Taiwan started in Oct, supporting the Oct revenues MoM increase (+21% MoM), followed by Vietnam capacity expansion in 1Q26, and US capacity expansion in mid 2026. During SC25 (Super Computing Conference 2025, link) in Nov, Mitac showcased their AI servers across training and inferencing, across liquid cooled and air cooled, and across NVIDIA and AMD platforms. We expect Mitac’s strong design capability and close work with its major US CSP customer, could support its business expansion, from networking racks to AI servers, driving its OP income to grow at 33% CAGR in 2025-28E post the turn around in 2024A, supporting the valuation re-rating. Maintain Buy.
