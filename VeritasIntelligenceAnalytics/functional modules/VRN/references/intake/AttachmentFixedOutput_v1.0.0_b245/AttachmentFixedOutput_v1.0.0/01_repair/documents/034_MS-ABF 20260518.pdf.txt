We raise our pricing and margin assumptions for ABF substrates.
We reiterate OW on Unimicron and NYPCB and raise PT, but also upgrade ZDT to OW, as our latest bottom-up ABF S/D model points to a more severe shortage by 2030.
Key Takeaways We estimate the S/D gap will widen to 22% by 2030 (vs 15% previously), driven by increased demand for GPUs, ASICs, and CPUs.
This will drive stronger pricing and margin tailwinds for ABF substrate suppliers.
Unimicron PT NT$1,225 (0.48x/0.24x CY27/28 PEG).
NYPCB PT NT$1,275 (0.50x/0.22x CY27/28 PEG).
Upgrade ZDT to OW with new PT of NT$570.
Stronger AI demand and more severe undersupply is driving higher pricing and margin assumptions for ABF substrates: We upgraded Unimicron and NYPCB on Feb 22, 2026 (report here). Over the past three months, both stocks have outperformed the TAIEX, up 123% and 89%, respectively, vs. the TAIEX up 23% over the same period. However, we don't think it's time to take profits, as we continue to see increasing demand, causing further supply constraints and a widening supply/ demand gap. Recent checks indicate that ABF undersupply gap would widen to 22%
by 2030 (vs 15% previously), which is putting upward pressure on pricing and margins for the substrate suppliers. Thus, we raise our CY26-28 EPS estimates for both stocks, which drives up our PTs for Unimicron to NT$1,225 (49% upside) and NYPCB to NT$1,275 (62% upside).
Increasing constraints are also driving a broadening out of the supply ecosystem:
As demand gets stronger, we are seeing end-customers choosing to allocate more demand to smaller scale suppliers, as well as customers choosing to work with new entrants.
