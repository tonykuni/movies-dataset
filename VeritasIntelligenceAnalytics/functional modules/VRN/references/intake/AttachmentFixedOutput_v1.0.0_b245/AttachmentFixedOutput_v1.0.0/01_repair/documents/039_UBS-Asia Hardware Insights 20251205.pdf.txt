The AI thermal wall: liquid cooling is the new standard As genAI scales, silicon power density exceeding 1,000W TDP is driving the adoption of liquid-enabled thermal management. Direct-to-chip liquid cooling (DLC) is now a critical infrastructure requirement for leading-edge AI racks in the Nvidia Grace Blackwell/Vera Rubin era and beyond. A bifurcated direct liquid cooling roadmap: micro channel lid and cold plate We see a market divided in two by power density. Micro-channel lids (MCL) – integrated directly into the heatspreader with the lid atop the silicon – are set to serve applications above 3,500W TDP. We believe adoption of MCL could start minimally in Q426E with overclocked NVIDIA VR200 (2,300W) and potentially become the standard for VR300 (3,600W). We estimate TAM for MCL at US$35m/US$1.25bn in 2026E/27E, reaching US $2.7bn in 2030E.
