Airtac International Group (1590.TW): Nov Monthly revenue +4% vs. GSe; strong broad-based inventory build-up demand
On Dec 2nd, AirTAC reported November revenue of Rmb691mn (+10% MoM, +21%
YoY) / NT$3,025mn (+12% MoM, +18% YoY), which was 4% higher than GSe in RMB.
In November, AirTAC’s revenue was 4% higher than GSe in RMB, mainly driven by (1)
solid CAPEX demand from the electronics industry, especially from iPhone supply chain (2) battery-related demand was stronger than expected in November, considering the ongoing import substitution trend, and (3) inventory build up demand from all end markets before year-end stock taking.
On the other hand, November revenue went up 21% YoY in RMB, despite 4% YoY fewer working days vs. November 2024. This reflects a slowdown from the peak level in October (22% YoY), but is still much faster than the YT11M YoY growth of 15%. We believe the solid revenue YoY growth was driven by (1) solid automotive and battery demand, which has continued to deliver high YoY revenue growth in the past few quarters, (2) recovering electronics demand, thanks to better iPhone supply chain CAPEX outlook, (3) broad-based demand recovery as we see all segment revenues growing 5%+ YoY despite 4% fewer working days (vs. last November), which we believe is in line with the company’s commentary on improving investment confidence in China (see here). We see increasing confidence around investment in capital goods from China end customers, but we haven’t really seen any significant improvement in the overall non-Semi/AI/EV industries’ demand in 2026, so we will closely monitor the demand outlook in the coming months/quarters.
In terms of growth by end-market segments for November, the battery sector continued to outgrow all other sectors (+68% YoY), followed by automotive (+25%
YoY), machine tool (+23% YoY), packaging (+14% YoY), electronics (+13% YoY), textile (12% YoY), general machinery (+10% YoY) and energy lighting (+8% YoY).
