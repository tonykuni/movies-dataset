Hon Hai (2317.TW): Nov Rev +26% YoY; AI server continued shipment growth; Buy (on CL)
Hon Hai’s Nov revenues +26% YoY/ -6% MoM to NT$844bn. Measured in USD, Nov revenues were +30% YoY/ -7% MoM, per management. From a MoM perspective, Component & Others had the strongest growth due to strong pull-in momentum. PC revenues were flat MoM, followed by the flattish Cloud & Networking segment. Management noted that AI server rack shipment continue to grow, while Nov revenues were impacted by (1) diversified transaction model in AI servers, and (2) other products in the segment including general servers and switch;
Consumer electronics saw MoM decline due to high base in Oct which was driven by holiday pull-ins. From a YoY perspective, Cloud & Networking and Component & Others showed strong YoY growth, while Consumer electronics were flattish YoY and PC declined slightly YoY due to slower order pull-ins. We expect Dec revenues to decline sequentially, due to the high base last month and slower seasonality towards year-end. However, we expect the continuous ramp-up of AI server with market share gain opportunities, along with the upcoming smartphone form factor changes in 2026E to drive Hon Hai’s growth ahead. Maintain Buy on CL.
