Business Extension to Power AI Energy infrastructure (SST, fuel cell, modular power solution, BESS) has been the key development focus. We are encouraged to see initial progress for 800 VDC-related offerings. The contribution might not turn meaningful from 2027, but this shows its extended TAM for powering AI. OW.
Key Takeaways Data center-related revenue is expected to be >40% of overall 2025 revenue and at least 50% in 2026. The offerings are margin-accretive at the operating level.
Energy infrastructure is the long-term business driver for its cohesive power solution offerings that fit into 800 VDC power architecture upgrade at data center.
We keep our earnings estimates and price target unchanged for now but we see potential upside to our mid-term growth rate of 15% once these turns materialize.
The stock trades at 26.6x 2026e P/E, attractive to data center infra peers Vertiv at 32.6x and Bloom Energy 40.2x.
First SST project wins to the data center: At the 2025 CDCC Summit on November 24, Delta announced its first solid-state transformer (SST) project for data centers together with Meituan and Chindata in China. The SST features 98.5% conversion efficiency with output power up to 1MW for an area size of one square meter, >50%
space saving. It could design for 240V/400V/800V DC power architecture and smart adjustment for GPU load volatility.
Fuel cell production kicks off in 2H26: Delta has been working on the development of these for >20 years. It licensed from Ceres Power in the UK for both SOFC and SOEC technology in January 2024. The production will be located in Taiwan initially with pilot run starting from 2H26. This is part of its cohesive power solution offerings for DC (direct current) power architecture, in addition to smart micro grid management and energy storage system. That helps to off-load the burden from Tower to distributed power grid for data centers.
