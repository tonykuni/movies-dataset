M October 7, 2025 01:32 PM GMT Wiwynn Corp | Asia Pacific Potentially positive developments for ASIC server business Our supply chain checks indicate potentially positive developments for Wiwynn's ASIC server business. Currently it is a major supplier for Amazon's ASIC server racks, but our checks indicate that Wiwynn may have seen positive developments in collaborating with Amazon (covered by Brian Nowak) in board-level assembly, UBB (universal baseboard) or L6 assembly, with production potentially in Malaysia and/ or Taiwan. Neither company has commented.
Potential implications for Wiwynn: The board-level business carries low ASP but high margin, so while any such development would not have a meaningful impact on the top line, it could be beneficial to margins. We recently downgraded Wiwynn to EW (report here), because we believed ASIC server rack shipments would peak in 3Q and enter a transition period in 4Q25-2Q26 before the next generation ramps in 2H26. If Wiwynn is able to enter the UBB business with Amazon, this could be a positive for Wiwynn stock.
