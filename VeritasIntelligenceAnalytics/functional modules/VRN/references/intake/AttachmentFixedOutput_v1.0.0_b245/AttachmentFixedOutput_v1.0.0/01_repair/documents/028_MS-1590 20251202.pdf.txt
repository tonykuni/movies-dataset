M December 2, 2025 02:18 PM GMT AirTAC International | Asia Pacific November Sales Up 12% MoM, 18% YoY Details:
• November sales came in at NT$3,025mn (+12% MoM/+18% YoY), supported by normalized working days and higher conviction on market recovery from major customers amid continuous government policy support and alleviated trade tensions.
• QTD revenue reached 69% of MSe at NT$8,345mn (+1% QoQ/+9% YoY) and consensus of NT$8,462mn (+3% QoQ/+11% YoY).
• Shipments from the battery, automotive, and machine tool segments saw stronger growth during the month.
Our view:
• We think its 4Q25 revenue is tracking ahead of market expectations.
• Management indicated that it has turned more optimistic on the outlook for the rest of this year and believes the momentum might extend into 2026.
• We think near-term sentiment should turn more favorable alongside its longer-term share gain story, especially as we view valuation as undemanding at 20x 2026 P/E (vs. mid-cycle average of 25x).
