Quanta (2382.TW): Sep revenue: -3% MoM likely due to weak general sever shipments, while PC shipments up 2% MoM; Buy
Goldman囤ity Sachs I Research Quanta reported Sep revenue at NT$98bn (-3% MoM) and notebook PC shipments up 2% MoM, leading to 3Q revenue of NT$287bn (6% / 4% below GSe and Bloomberg consensus). We think the MoM decline was mainly due to weak general servers shipments, while AI servers were ramping up. In October, we expect slower PC shipments than Sept given some orders were pulled in earlier, but shipments of GPU servers should support 4Q23 revenues to recover QoQ. We are positive on Quanta on the AI server uptrend and PC market recovery. We view AI servers as long-term uptrend as generative AI applications continue to emerge, including Meta’s GenAI application on mixed reality headsets / smart glasses and Google’s on smartphones. Maintain Buy.
