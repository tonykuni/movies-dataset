M November 26, 2025 02:24 PM GMT Greater China Technology Hardware | Asia Pacific Automation – Read-across from JMTBA October 2025 Orders Per the Japan Machine Tool Builders' Association (JMTBA), headline orders rose 3% MoM and 17% YoY in October 2025 to ¥143bn.
• Domestic orders -19% MoM/+6% YoY to ¥36bn
• Overseas orders +13% MoM/+21% YoY to ¥108bn
• By region:
° China: -7% MoM/+10% YoY to ¥32bn° Europe: +30% MoM/+22% YoY to ¥20bn° North America: +14% MoM/+58% YoY to ¥34bn Our view:
• Orders from China have held up well on a YoY basis despite the sequential decline on seasonality; meanwhile, we are seeing more signs of sustainable recovery in North America and Europe orders.
• Among Taiwan automation names. we prefer Airtac over Hiwin, as Airtac should continue to see share gains with undemanding valuation of 20x 2026e P/E (vs. average of 25x since 2020). The combination of lukewarm growth and hefty valuation (29x 2026e P/E now vs. average of 26x since 2020) leads to unattractive risk-reward for Hiwin.
