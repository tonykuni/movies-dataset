Anticipating another round of upward EPS revisions3Q23 EPS hits record high in light of upbeat marginsOrder outlook remains robust for upcoming peak seasonReaffirming our Buy (1) call; lifting 12-month TP to TWD100 What’s new: Tong Yang released its 9M23 operating data on 11 October, having released its 9M23 revenue data on 4 October (see our memo). We believe the increasing adoption of after-market (AM) components in the US is likely to make it a structural trend, which we see as a mid-to-long-term revenue driver. However, in the near term, we believe the upward EPS revision trend will be the share-price catalyst. We reaffirm our Buy (1) call.
What’s the impact: Record-high quarterly EPS in 3Q23, mainly thanks to revenue, gross margin and operating margin beat. Tong Yang’s 3Q23 revenue of TWD6,154m (+10.9% QoQ and +11.3% YoY) was ahead of our forecast and the consensus estimate by 6.1% and 4.8%, respectively, mainly due to the solid AM revenue and recovery in China OEM revenue. Its operating margin came in at 16.5% in 3Q23, ahead of our (14.9%) and the consensus (14.7%) forecasts. Its operating profit of TWD1,018m (+27.8% QoQ and +82.4% YoY) beat both our and the consensus forecasts by 17.6%, which we attribute to the upbeat gross margin from the AM business with improving operating leverage. With an FX gain of TWD187.4m, Tong Yang’s pre-tax profit of TWD1,238m (+30.4%
QoQ and +43.4% YoY) beat our and the consensus estimates by 16.9%
and 23.3%, respectively. Its gross margin was c.30% in 3Q23 (vs. our forecast of 28.9% and the consensus forecast of 28.8%), up from 29.0% in 2Q23 and 24.6% in 3Q22.
