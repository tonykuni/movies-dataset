will.chang@daiwacm-cathay.com.tw Stacy Lin (886) 2 8758 6252 What’s new?
1. PCIe 7 poised to trigger an unprecedented leap in HVLP demand Beyond AI servers, in terms of regular server CPU motherboards, the mainstream specs in 2025-26 will remain PCIe Gen 5-6, mostly using RTF2-4 grade copper foil. Looking ahead, with higher data rate and frequency requirements, we expect PCIe Gen 7 to undergo a meaningful spec upgrade, adopting at least HVLP3 grade copper foil (including HVLP3/4).
With annual demand of around 15m regular server CPU motherboards and each motherboard typically requiring at least 2kg of copper foil, we estimate that once the transition to PCIe 7 is complete, coupled with continued layer count migration (to 24–30L), HVLP3/4 demand could reach at least 30k tonnes annually (=~2,500 tonnes/month).
Therefore, we believe a sizable HVLP demand surge is likely to start from 2028.
Meanwhile, aside from CCL suppliers actively securing HVLP3/4 copper foil now, we also observe PCB suppliers facing shortages, as HDI build-up processes require HVLP3/4 copper foil as well and are typically Toz (=1/3 oz). However, given tight HVLP supply, copper foil makers are relatively reluctant to produce Toz due to ~3x longer manufacturing time relative to Hoz (=1/2 oz) without proportional pricing gains.
Overall, we expect HVLP tightness to deepen into 2026, especially post AWS Trainium product transition, and anticipate the next HVLP price hike to materialize in 1H26.
