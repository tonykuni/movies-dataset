Asia Communacopia + Technology — Key Takeaways: More capacity to come in 2028, with substrate CCL the main focus
Elite Material (2383.TW)
2383.TW 12m Price Target: NT$6,000.00 Price: NT$4,545.00 Upside: 32.0%
We hosted EMC during our Asia Communacopia + Technology conference (May 18-19) in Hong Kong. Key takeaways include:
(1) Revise up 2028 capacity target by +20%: The company revised up its 2028 target capacity from 950mn sheets/month in 2027 to 1,150mn/month, implying a 21% increase (but please note that this is just a target for now, and is waiting for BOD approval). With PCB clients expect to expand capacity +2x over the next two years, and given PCB suppliers are central to the supply chain, CCL suppliers are likely to align their actions with PCB capacity expansion plans.
(2) EMC leads in mSAP solutions and target to enter the substrate industry with good confidence: The mSAP (substrate like PCB) solution was first adopted by smartphones, where EMC has maintained a dominant share (~80%) since 2019. The company has been developing mSAP-based solutions for CPO applications for over two years and expect the technology to mature within the next two years, with a subsequent ramp up in volume. Moreover, the company is targeting to enter the substrate CCL industry proactively in coming years (considering the company’s good technology skill set on substrate like CCL), which could be the next growing engine for earnings for next decade (GM for substrate CCL is much higher than PCB CCL, with high entry barrier).
(3) Price hike reflecting rising material costs will materialize in 2Q26E: Despite seeing a sharp increase in raw material (including copper foil, low-DK, E-glass, etc), the company only started to pass through price hikes from April (lagging peers) with broad acceptance from major clients, and expect 2Q26 to show margin recovery.
