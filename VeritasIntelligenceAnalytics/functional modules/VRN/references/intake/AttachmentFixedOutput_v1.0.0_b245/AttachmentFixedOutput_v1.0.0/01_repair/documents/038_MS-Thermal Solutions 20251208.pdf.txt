M December 8, 2025 12:26 PM GMT Greater China Technology Hardware | Asia Pacific Thermal Solutions – Record Revenue in November Greater China Technology Hardware Asia Pacific Industry View In-Line We are encouraged to see ramping liquid cooling component shipments in November and expect the strength to carry into 1Q26. However, the growth magnitude showed a diverse trend from our expectations. We thus hold to our preference that we prefer AVC (OW) and Fositek (OW) over Auras (EW).
Asia Vital Components (AVC, 3017.TW) – November revenue of NT$17,382mn (+6% MoM, +142% YoY) reached a record high and was 6% above our estimate:
This reflects ongoing cold plate module shipment ramps of GB300 server racks, cold plate shipment rollout for ASIC AI servers, despite smartphone VC shipments were relatively stable MoM. We estimate that AVC's 4Q25 revenue will grow 25%
QoQ, to NT$48.5bn (up 132% YoY). We see potential upside to our and consensus 4Q25 revenue forecasts as October and November revenue combined already amount to 70% of our 4Q25 revenue estimate.
Fositek (6805.TW) – November revenue of NT$1,483mn (+14% MoM, +47% YoY)
reached a record high and was 7% above our estimate: The upside is mainly supported by smooth ramp of GB300-related shipments. We estimate that Fositek's 4Q25 revenue will grow 12% QoQ to NT$4,002mn (up 44% YoY). We see potential upside to our and consensus 4Q25 revenue forecasts.
Auras (3324.TWO) – November revenue also hit a record high at NT$2,444mn (+5% MoM, +78% YoY), but 5% below our estimate: The slower-than-expected monthly sales ramp mainly reflects slower-than-expected pull-in due to Thanksgiving holidays. Despite lower October and November revenue, company believes 4Q25 revenue guidance of 25% QoQ growth is still achievable. That implies December revenue would grow 12% MoM.
Looking into 1Q26, revenue outlook is unchanged, with January shipment expected to grow sequentially.
