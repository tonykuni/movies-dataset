On Nov 25, Bloomberg reported that Meta is in talks to use Google’s TPU chips in 2027 following prior news that Meta might use Google’s GCP cloud services next year.
Comments ASIC to external customers: We believe Custom AI accelerators (ASIC), including the Google TPU and Amazon Trainium, continue to scale as these platforms support both internal AI workloads and external customers. As said in our Broadcom report (2026 TPU CoWoS Better than Expected on Oct 16), we expect TPU volume to be driven by external customers such as Anthropic, xAI, Meta, Apple, etc, while we believe the major volume to be from Anthropic, at the expense of Trainium, starting 2026. That said, hyperscalers such as Microsoft, Meta and Oracle remain reliant on Nvidia platforms (and also on AMD).
Google to increase the demand for Nvidia GPU: Despite the TPU ramp-up, we believe that major server ODMs have seen increased demand from Google for Nvidia’s VR200 platform in 2H26. We note that Google was/is a small buyer for NVL72 solution; however, we believe that Rubin’s performance will be much ahead of TPU v7’s (in 4Q25) and even v8’s (in 4Q26), judging from v8’s inferior spec of HBM and smaller reticle size. Therefore, we expect Nvidia’s volume to Google to grow into 2026 and 2027.
We like both, merchant GPUs and TPU supply chain: According to AMD’s CEO Dr. Lisa Su on Nov 11 2025, she expects the datacenter silicon market to reach $1 trillion by 2030, compared to her earlier expectation of AI accelerator market to reach $500bn by 2028. Based on our forecast, we forecast AI accelerator market to grow by 59% YoY/44% YoY in 2026E/2027E.
