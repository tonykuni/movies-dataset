Strong May-25 Sales Point to Solid GB200 Ramp and Potential Earnings Upside; Reiterate Buy CITI’S TAKE Wistron’s reported May-25 sales of NT$208.4bn (+56% m-o-m/+162% yo-y) beat expectations, with the combined Apr-25 and May-25 sales reaching c.83%/c.86% of Citi/cons 2Q25E sales forecast, pointing to possible upside. While Wiwynn has yet to release its May-25 sales number, we believe the smoother ramp-up of GB200 for Dell was the key driver and expect the momentum to continue. Mgmt. had previously guided to delivery of compute trays equivalent to 500-1,000 GB200 racks in May-25 and June-25, and the ramp-up seems to be on track. Despite potential shipment variability in 2H25E due to the nonlinear nature of large-scale AI infrastructure deployment, we see meaningful upside to consensus – especially as Wistron continues to add new customers/projects including Oracle and Cisco. We expect the shares to react positively; the fact that mgmt. guided for GB200 compute tray profitability to exceed that of the NB business should help assuage investors’ concerns.
