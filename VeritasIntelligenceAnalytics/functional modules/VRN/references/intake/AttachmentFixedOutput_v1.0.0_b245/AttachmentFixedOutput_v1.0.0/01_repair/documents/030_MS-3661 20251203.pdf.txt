M December 3, 2025 08:17 PM GMT Alchip Technologies Ltd | Asia Pacific Read-across from AWS re:Invent and Marvell results; keep OW Trainium3's revenue contribution to Alchip should stay intact, while Alchip should be able to support both UALink and NVLink for Trainium4. Reiterate OW – accumulate on dips in particular.
Alchip stock was down 7% yesterday vs. the Taiex up 1%. We cite two factors:
1. Alchip plays a key role in design service for both the Trainium3 system and Trainium4 chips. There could have been profit-taking after AWS re:Invent yesterday, when the Trainium3 system and Trainium4 chip architecture were announced.
2. Key competitor Marvell (covered by Joe Moore) guided for ASIC business to grow in 2026. Investors are also concerned that Marvell’s ASIC growth in 2026 and 2027 could be at the expense of Alchip.
The concern around Marvell's competition for AWS XPU business lingers: While we understand investors' concerns, in Joe’s report, he indicated, “In CY26 we think that the 20% growth is realistic, and comes mostly from XPU attach opportunities, with (per management) minimal contribution from the next big ASIC opportunity”.
We suggest building positions on dips given Alchip's potential 2026 revenue upside from Trainium3 and its higher revenue content in the Trainium4 product cycle.
What about Trainium3 news at re:Invent or from the AWS announcement? We believe Alchip remains the only true design service provider for Trainium3 because it finished the computing die and CoWoS-R circuit designs in July. Some investors have asked whether the potential 3nm wafer supply from Broadcom and Marvell for Trainium will one day turn into real business for Trainium4 and negatively affect Alchip. We think differently and expect the stock to re-rate once Alchip’s earnings grow with AWS’s Trainium4 demand during 2027-29.
