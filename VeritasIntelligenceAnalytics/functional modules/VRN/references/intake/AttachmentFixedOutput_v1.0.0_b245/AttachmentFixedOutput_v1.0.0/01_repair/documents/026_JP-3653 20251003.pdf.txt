Key feedback and FAQs from investors In this note, we address the key FAQs from investors on Jentech after we initiated coverage on September 29 (link).
• What is already priced into Jentech’s current share price? What is not yet factored-in which could drive further outperformance in its share price?
Despite Jentech’s share price being up ~60% YTD vs Taiex’s 15%, we don’t sense that the market has priced in the opportunities of micro-channel lid to AI chips adoption soon. This is based on the majority feedback from our buy side customers, and their consensus is that micro-channel lid will be adopted in 2H27 at the earliest. Second, micro-channel lid is a product that can replace the current cold plate module, but AVC (3017.TW), the biggest cold plate module maker’s share price is up ~80% YTD. We are more constructive vs. the Street on the adoption of micro-channel lid to start from 2H26 and become more mainstream in 2027 (details in below paragraphs), while Jentech will be the sole supplier in the first year of adoption and can still maintain its leadership in the upcoming years, given its solid technology leadership in the packaginglevel cooling solutions, especially for AI server chips. We believe any further confirmation in the upcoming months will be a major share price driver. On the other hand, we believe Jentech is one of the largest beneficiaries of the compute tray design upgrade in the next-gen AI server system, driven by the additional chips placed in the tray (for inference processing), as each incremental chip will require a lid as well, in addition to the existing AI GPUs and CPUs.
