Data center and auto segments set to fuel next earnings upcycle; Upgrade to Buy; TP up to NT$650
Silergy Corp. (6415.TW)
6415.TW 12m Price Target: NT$650.00 Price: NT$468.00 Upside: 38.9%
Dual growth engines moving into focus We are turning more constructive on Silergy and upgrading the stock to Buy from Neutral, as we believe the company is entering a new earnings upcycle driven by 1) stronger-than-expected data centers demand, which we expect to contribute 15-20% of revenue in 2026E (vs. less than 10% in 2025), with further upside into 2027/2028, 2) continued growth in automotive content, with Silergy’s dollar content per vehicle expected to more than double in 2026, which in our view should drive auto revenue growth of more than 50% YoY this year, and 3) higher structural GM, supported by more favorable product mix and rising penetration of Gen 4 products, underpinned by strength in auto and data center applications. Overall, we revise up our 2026/2027E earnings by 35-61% and expect 72% earnings CAGR in 2025-2028E.
Growth is now back on track We believe analog is entering a new upcycle, with demand expanding beyond a purely cyclical rebound. Silergy’s growth profile is shifting from a consumer-driven recovery toward a structural story underpinned by auto and data center demand. Management is now confident of achieving at least 20% YoY revenue growth in 2026, returning to its long-term 20-30% growth target after a subdued 2025 (revenue up only 1.9% YoY). Management also highlighted data center and automotive segments as the company’s main growth drivers, with new product dollar content ramping faster than expected, supported by higher chip power consumption and strong EV/hybrid EV demand.
Net net, we now forecast Silergy’s 2026-2028E revenue to grow 25%/32%/28% YoY, with GM improving to 51.0%/54.8%/55.5%.
Following our earnings revisions, we raise our 12m TP to NT$650 from NT$310 (30x 2027E EPS), which implies c.39% upside — we upgrade to Buy from Neutral.
