M December 4, 2025 08:00 PM GMT Greater China Semiconductors | Asia Pacific Old memory: keeps surprising to the upside We believe the pricing power of DDR4, MLC NAND, and NOR is getting even stronger into 2026. We reiterate our bullish view on all our covered old memory stocks. Top Pick Winbond.
Key Takeaways DDR4: Demand from enterprise customers could easily push contract pricing up 100%+ in 1Q.
NOR Flash: Supply adjustment could push NOR pricing up 20%+ in 1Q.
MLC NAND: Sharp supply disruption may cause significant pricing hike in 1Q.
Not the time to take profits: Starting from late 2Q, we had expected that old memory undersupply would drive a super-cycle (link). We also built our own DDR4, SLC, MLC, and NOR Flash model (link). Old memory contract pricing started to pick up in late 3Q. A pure old memory up-cycle usually lasts for 3-4 quarters, which suggests that it's not the time to take profit now. Consensus earnings could increase very meaningfully in 2026.
Could there be more DDR4 upside? Yes: The preliminary pricing negotiations for 1Q26 look even more favorable for suppliers. The major reason is the demand coming from enterprises (link), which could push contract pricing up >100% in 1Q26.
Meanwhile, DDR4 16Gb is in the most significant shortage. We believe inventory in channels is close to zero, with spot pricing as high as US$100, compared to spot price of US$45.5 according to DRAMeXchange.
The supply cut is driving up all the Flash products: The supply cut in MLC NAND is a big issue (link), and now we also expect some NOR cuts as well. Given the low wafer value and back-end pricing hike, we believe some NOR suppliers could reallocate their NOR capacity to other products. We thus expect NOR undersupply in 2026 could widen to high single digits from low to mid single digits. NOR pricing could go up >20% in 1Q26.
