We prefer AVC over Auras.

Asia Vital Components (AVC, 3017.TW) – September revenue of NT$14,503mn (+15% MoM, +128% YoY) reached a record high and was 23% above our estimate:
This reflects the start of shipments of cold plate modules for the computing tray of GB300 server racks and smartphone VC shipments.
4Q25 revenue should continue to grow 10-20% QoQ (up 105-123% YoY) with the ongoing ramp of cold plate module shipments for GB300 server racks and new ASIC AI server project rollouts in 4Q25.
Auras (3324.TWO) – September revenue also hit a record high at NT$2,340mn (+25% MoM, +64% YoY): This mainly reflects liquid cooling shipment momentum.
The company believes 4Q25 revenue is set for 25% QoQ growth (up >81% YoY), with server revenue contribution expected to reach 70% of total revenue.
