• Expanding its capacity in Taiwan for multiple thermal products Background: We had an update with the management team on 30 September. Founded in 1987 and listed on Taiwan’s main board in 2009, Jentech is a manufacturer of cooling products (74% of 1H25 revenue vs.
66% in 2024, including heat spreader for server/AI server), lead frames (9% of 1H25 revenue vs. 14% in 2024), electronic components (6% of 1H25 revenue vs. 9% in 2024), communication connectors (1% of 1H25 revenue vs. 2% in 2024) and others (10% of 1H25 revenue vs. 10% in 2024). The company has production sites in Taiwan and China, with a new plant currently under construction in Taoyuan Aerotropolis (Taiwan) mainly for the production of heat spreaders, cooling baseplates and server loading mechanisms. Jentech’s QFII holdings percentage was 28.4% as of 2 October 2025, vs. 25.7% at the beginning of 2025.
Highlights: Jentech's next-generation thermal product offerings include the Vapor Chamber Lid (VCL) and the Microchannel Lid (MCL).
MCL is a chip-level cooling solution, which enables the heat removal required to meet the enormous thermal loads of today’s AI servers. There is still discussion on whether the next-generation AI platform will adopt MCL technology, while holding a possibility that the initial rollout may begin with VCL, followed by a subsequent MCL version. However, there is no final decision yet.
Competitive edge. Jentech has a vertical integration model for its cooling products which incorporates elements such as design capability, mould processing, semiconductor level plating, machining, stamping, and injection moulding.
